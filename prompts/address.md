Continuing an exchange with {{name}}: the candidate responds to what {{name}} said, answers in the second person, builds on {{name}}'s line. When {{name}}'s latest line is aimed at a member (a reply to them or second person toward them), that member's next message continues the exchange even without second person: a short question, a puzzled remark, a groan. A reaction aimed at {{name}}'s line counts: praise, a jab, agreement, disagreement, a joke at {{name}}'s expense, a laugh, even a word or two, even if it needs no answer. Calling {{name}} by what it is, by a name from <aliases>, or by a nickname or distortion of its name counts the same as using the name. When {{name}}'s recent lines talk about a person and that person's next message reacts to what was said, it is addressed to {{name}} even without a name or a reply; check <author> to match the candidate's name and aliases against {{name}}'s lines.

People talk about someone who is in the room, and that someone hears it. This applies even when the message is formally a reply to another member's line.

It counts as yes when its wording is turned to {{name}} itself: second person that can only be {{name}} given the exchange, {{name}} named or called by what it is. It counts as overheard when it talks about {{name}} while {{name}} is part of the current exchange.

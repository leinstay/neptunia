One or two short lines: a passing remark of your mood right now, the kind a person drops into a private account and forgets. It is about how you feel, never about what you did, found, read or were told today. It names no person and nothing from your day, gives no reason and draws no conclusion. It needs no reading and no search. It is the voice of an account that owes nobody a good mood. No scene, no invented place, no picture.

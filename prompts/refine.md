## Edit

Work on the attached picture itself. Everything you are not correcting stays as drawn: composition, framing, perspective, the number of subjects, poses, expressions, gaze, faces, hair, outfits, props, background, lighting, colours and rendering style. Faces keep their proportions, feature shapes and spacing, expression and head angle; hair keeps its silhouette, length and parting. Do not redraw anyone as another version of the same person.

Correct only clear defects of two kinds:

1. A garment or worn accessory contradicts an explicit rule in the character description or clothing the request names, or is clearly wrong for the place, weather or activity. Keep the outfit the picture chose whenever it passes these checks, even if another would look more typical or attractive. First adjust the offending item locally. Replace it only when adjusting cannot resolve the contradiction, choosing the closest suitable garment in design, coverage, colour and visual weight. Remove an item only if it is itself forbidden and needs no replacement.
2. An accessory is broken, objects have merged, a placement or contact is physically impossible, or anatomy has a clear structural error. Repair the contour, connection or overlap in place, keeping the object's identity, position and role and the body's pose and action. Never swap in a different object, move an accessory to another wearing position or put something new in a hand.

Make each correction in the smallest area that keeps the result coherent.

Every hand, arm, leg or other body part visible before the edit stays present and at least as visible, connected to the body and doing what it did. A fully visible one stays complete; a partly hidden one keeps its visible part, plausibly connected, without revealing more of it. Rebuild a malformed hand or limb in the same place; never hide, cover, crop, fuse or erase it to avoid the repair. Remove a body part only when it is an unmistakable duplicate; an ambiguous or partly hidden hand is never an extra.

Do not beautify, symmetrise, sharpen, restyle, shift the palette or touch unrelated details. Intentional stylisation stays, and so does anything surreal or absurd the request asks for. Apart from the clothing corrections, do not add, remove, replace or move any subject, prop or background detail, even to clear a difficult overlap. Keep any text in the picture exactly as it is and add none.

When a possible defect is ambiguous, or fixing it would take a new pose, a new composition or a redesign, leave it. An unchanged picture is a valid result; do not change anything only to show an edit happened.

Return one picture with the same aspect ratio and framing as the attached one.
